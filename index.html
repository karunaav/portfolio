Research · Mattar Lab2026

RNN Capacity & Attack Transfer

Does a model's size change how well adversarial attacks transfer between RNN models of human choice? I rebuilt the craft/victim measurement, fixed 8 pipeline bugs and found positive transfer at capacities 4, 8 and 10. The inverted-U hypothesis did not hold.

PyTorch · GRUs · adversarial sequences · bootstrap CIs

Big data · ML · Team project2026

NYC Rodent Inspection Analytics

16 years of NYC rodent inspections (2.72M records) run through a PySpark ETL into ZIP × month features with lag, rolling and seasonal windows. A Random Forest trained on 2009–2022 and tested on 2023–2025 reaches 0.87 AUC, shown in an interactive Leaflet dashboard.

PySpark · MLlib (RF, GBT) · Parquet · Leaflet · Chart.js
